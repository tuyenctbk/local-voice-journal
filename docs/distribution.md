# Google Play Store Listing & Distribution Details - AuraJournal

This document records the official Google Play Store store listing information, metadata, release names, and release notes for **AuraJournal**.

---

## 1. Store Listing Metadata (English Default)

* **App Title**: AuraJournal
* **Short Description** (Max 80 chars): Privacy-first local voice reflection journal & emotional insight tracker.
* **Full Description** (Max 4000 chars):
  ```text
  AuraJournal is a sensory-friendly, minimalist voice reflection space built to preserve your total privacy. Reflect on your day, track your habits, and gain emotional insights with a single tap—completely offline.

  By combining local speech-to-text transcription with on-device natural language heuristics, AuraJournal ensures your most personal reflections never leave your phone. No logins, no cloud servers, and no data tracking.

  KEY FEATURES:
  1. One-Tap Voice Logging: Record up to 60-second voice reflection sessions with sensory-friendly visual feedback.
  2. Offline Speech-to-Text: Real-time local transcription utilizing Android's system-level speech recognition without uploading audio bytes to external servers.
  3. On-Device AI Insights: Instantly extracts core themes (Career, Relationships, Fitness, sleep), stress levels (Low, Medium, High), and habit metrics locally.
  4. Weekly Trends Dashboard: Beautiful, glowing Compose Canvas trendlines and habit tracking charts (Premium).
  5. Local JSON Portability: Export and import secure JSON backup files directly on-device using the Android Share Sheet.
  6. Wear OS Companion: Capture voice entries on your watch that sync directly to your companion phone.
  7. Android Auto Support: Reflect safely during your daily commute.

  PRIVACY-FIRST DESIGN:
  AuraJournal operates 100% offline. The database rests locally in Room SQLite, and we utilize no central data logging servers. Your voice is yours alone.
  ```
